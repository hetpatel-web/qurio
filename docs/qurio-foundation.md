# Qurio Foundation

## Purpose

Help people prove to themselves that they can figure things out.

## Enemy

Passive helplessness — the belief that you need permission, certainty, or expertise before you can act.

## Mission

Qurio helps people move from passive consumption to active capability by turning curiosity into action, action into learning, and learning into confidence.

## Method

Question → Experiment → Build → Document → Reflect → Improve

## Tagline

Turn curiosity into capability.
